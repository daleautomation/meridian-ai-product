# MUTUAL NON-DISCLOSURE AGREEMENT

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement (the "**Agreement**") is entered into as of
________________, 2026 (the "**Effective Date**") by and between:

- **Meridian AI LLC**, a limited liability company, represented by **Dylan Dale** ("**Meridian**"); and
- **Lejla Ramic**, an individual ("**Recipient**").

Meridian and Recipient are each a "**Party**" and together the "**Parties**." This Agreement is
mutual: each Party may act as the "**Disclosing Party**" when it discloses information and as the
"**Receiving Party**" when it receives information.

## 1. Purpose
The Parties wish to explore a potential working relationship — specifically, the **technical
review and evaluation of Meridian's software, systems, and business** (the "**Purpose**"). In
connection with the Purpose, each Party may disclose certain confidential and proprietary
information to the other.

## 2. Definitions
"**Confidential Information**" means any non-public information disclosed by the Disclosing Party
to the Receiving Party, in any form (written, oral, electronic, visual, or by access to systems
or materials), that is marked or identified as confidential, or that a reasonable person would
understand to be confidential given its nature or the circumstances of disclosure.

## 3. Confidential Information
Confidential Information includes, without limitation: **source code and software; technical
architecture, designs, and documentation; business and commercialization strategy; product
plans and roadmaps; pricing and financial information; customer and prospect information;
proprietary methods, processes, and know-how;** and any notes, analyses, or materials derived
from the foregoing.

## 4. Exclusions
Confidential Information does not include information that the Receiving Party can demonstrate:
(a) is or becomes publicly available through no breach of this Agreement; (b) was rightfully
known to it, without obligation of confidentiality, before disclosure; (c) is rightfully
received from a third party without restriction; or (d) is independently developed without use
of or reference to the Disclosing Party's Confidential Information. Disclosure required by law
or valid court order is permitted, provided the Receiving Party gives prompt prior notice (where
lawful) and discloses only what is legally required.

## 5. Permitted Use
The Receiving Party may use the Confidential Information **solely for the Purpose**. The Receiving
Party may review Meridian's documentation, technical architecture, and source code **solely for
evaluation purposes**. No other use is permitted without the Disclosing Party's prior written
consent.

## 6. Non-Disclosure Obligations
The Receiving Party shall: (a) hold the Confidential Information in strict confidence; (b) not
disclose it to any third party; (c) protect it with at least the same degree of care it uses for
its own confidential information, and in no event less than reasonable care; and (d) limit use of
the Confidential Information to what is necessary for the Purpose. The Receiving Party shall
**not distribute, copy, reproduce, publish, or disclose** the Confidential Information or any
materials provided, except as expressly permitted in this Agreement.

## 7. No Access to Sensitive Systems
This Agreement grants **no right of access to any customer data, production credentials, API
keys, secrets, or live infrastructure**. No such access is provided by, or implied through, this
Agreement.

## 8. No License; No Ownership; No Equity
All Confidential Information remains the property of the Disclosing Party. Nothing in this
Agreement grants the Receiving Party any license or right (by implication or otherwise) to any
intellectual property, and **no ownership, equity, employment, partnership, or compensation of
any kind is created or implied** by access to the materials or by this Agreement.

## 9. Feedback
Any suggestions, feedback, recommendations, designs, ideas, or technical observations the
Receiving Party provides to Meridian in connection with the Purpose ("**Feedback**") **may be used
by Meridian AI LLC for any purpose without restriction, attribution, or compensation, unless
otherwise agreed in writing.**

## 10. Return or Destruction of Materials
Upon the Disclosing Party's written request, or upon termination of discussions, the Receiving
Party shall promptly **return or destroy** (at the Disclosing Party's option) all Confidential
Information and materials in its possession, including copies and derivatives, and, if requested,
certify such destruction in writing.

## 11. Term
This Agreement is effective as of the Effective Date, and the confidentiality obligations herein
shall remain in effect for **three (3) years** from the Effective Date.

## 12. Remedies
The Parties agree that a breach of this Agreement may cause irreparable harm for which monetary
damages are inadequate, and that the Disclosing Party shall be entitled to seek **injunctive
relief**, in addition to any other remedies available at law or in equity.

## 13. General
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the **State of Missouri**, without regard to its
conflict-of-laws principles. It constitutes the entire agreement between the Parties regarding
its subject matter and supersedes all prior understandings. It may be amended only in a writing
signed by both Parties. If any provision is held unenforceable, the remaining provisions remain
in full force. No waiver is effective unless in writing. This Agreement may be executed in
counterparts and by **electronic signature**, each of which is deemed an original and which
together constitute one instrument.
