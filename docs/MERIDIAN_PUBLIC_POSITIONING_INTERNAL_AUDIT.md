# Meridian Public Positioning Internal Audit

This document is internal-only product alignment for the public website cleanup. Do not publish it as marketing copy.

## What Meridian is actually strong at now

- Relationship prioritization: showing who matters, why now, and what should happen next.
- Stale opportunity recovery: turning old CRM records, estimates, proposals, referrals, and warm conversations into a ranked recovery queue.
- Workflow compression: reducing messy context into a small operator surface with priority, reason, owner, timing, and next action.
- Operator clarity: making the next move obvious without forcing users into a generic CRM replacement.
- Queue systems: presenting work as ordered daily execution instead of disconnected dashboards.
- Showcase generation: clean branded routes, vertical overlays, before/after panels, and screen-recording-safe layouts.
- Demo overlays: cinematic orange/blue visual language that can explain a workflow quickly.
- Execution guidance: call plans, recovery angles, owner-aware follow-up, and action framing.
- Follow-up systems: practical recovery views for stale pipeline and relationship continuity.
- Visual storytelling: turning abstract operations into demos buyers can understand.
- Relationship-to-revenue workflows: tying relationship attention to commercial movement.

## What is demoable and believable now

- Relationship-priority workspace and showcase mode.
- Clean showcase library routes and demo redirects.
- Vertical demo framing across roofing, restoration, home services, solar, recruiting, agency, insurance, real estate, SaaS sales, and advisors.
- Before/after clutter-to-clarity panels.
- Operator surfaces that explain who matters, why now, and what next.
- Public intake paths for scan and workspace requests.
- Premium public pages that position Meridian around relationship execution.

## What genuinely sells now

- Priority Scan for relationship and stale-pipeline diagnosis.
- CRM Recovery Scan for dormant opportunities.
- Follow-Up Recovery for owner, timing, and next-action clarity.
- Personal Relationship Queue for solo operators.
- Team Relationship Workspace for shared recovery and routing.
- Showcase/demo systems for explaining workflow value in sales conversations.
- Custom Operator Systems when the buyer has a clear workflow bottleneck and enough data/process maturity.

## Current product gaps and UX leaks

- Missing enrichment: public positioning implies useful context, but enrichment quality depends on available data and integrations.
- Weak integrations: CRM, email, calendar, phone, and enrichment connectors are not yet a polished public promise.
- Weak onboarding: intake exists, but the transition from public request to configured workspace is still under-defined.
- Unfinished action hooks: many actions are framed as next steps, but not every action is connected to a durable external execution channel.
- Weak follow-up automation: guidance is stronger than fully automated follow-up orchestration.
- Unclear transitions: users need clearer expectations between scan, workspace request, showcase demo, and live build.
- Dead-end UI areas: some public CTAs collect intent but do not yet provide a self-serve next screen.
- Conversion leaks: the product ladder is clearer now, but pricing, timelines, and package boundaries still need more operational definition.
- Overpromising risk: avoid implying full CRM replacement, full enrichment, or autonomous revenue recovery.
- Roofing gravity: existing routes and examples still make roofing visible; public homepage now needs to keep it as one example among many.

## Product direction guardrails

- Lead with relationships and revenue, not AI, infrastructure, dashboards, or one vertical.
- Keep demos honest: show what is working, frame roadmap items as roadmap, and avoid fake integrations.
- Sell the smallest useful system first.
- Make single-user systems and shared workspaces commercially distinct.
- Preserve the showcase library as a main sales engine.
- Keep public pages fast, readable, low-friction, and screen-recording-safe.
