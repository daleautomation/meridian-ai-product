# What Meridian Does — Customer Expectations

> A short doc to share with the operator BEFORE the first Monday
> brief. Sets the expectation in plain language so they don't expect
> something the product doesn't do.

---

## What you'll receive

Every Monday at 7 AM, an email from the Meridian founder. Three lines.
A link to your workspace.

Click the link. Your workspace shows up to eight priority
relationships for the week. Each one names the CRM evidence behind
why it's there — a tag, a last contact date, a note you wrote
yourself months ago. No prediction. No probability. Just the
material from your own CRM, surfaced and ranked.

For each priority, you'll see a suggested opener — also derived from
your CRM material. The opener isn't AI-written; it cites a specific
phrase from your notes or a tag you applied, and recommends opening
with that.

Under each priority are five outcome buttons: **Sent**, **No
answer**, **Meeting booked**, **Follow up later**, **Wrong contact**.
After you reach out, click one. That's it.

---

## What it does NOT do

- It does not send messages for you.
- It does not predict who's about to buy / sell / refi.
- It does not score people as "hot" or "cold."
- It does not replace your CRM.
- It does not surface new prospects you don't already know.
- It does not work in real time. The brief is generated Sunday
  night; the workspace is a Monday surface.

---

## What changes after a few weeks

In Week 1, you'll likely see priorities backed by older context — a
tag from 2023, a last contact from 2022. The workspace will tell
you honestly: "Continuity insights begin after your first week of
captured outcomes."

In Week 2's brief, the priorities reflect what you did in Week 1.
A contact you marked "No answer" drops to the bottom of the slice.
A contact you marked "Meeting booked" disappears from the list
entirely.

In Week 3's brief, the workspace starts naming specific contacts
whose Week 1 + Week 2 outcomes are reshaping this week's priorities.
*"Last week you captured 4 outcomes. 2 of them (Brendan, Phil) shape
this week's priorities."*

By Week 8, the workspace has memory of two months of your decisions.
That memory does what no CRM does today: it links forward and
backward in time so you don't repeat yourself or forget a thread.

---

## What we ask of you

- **Open the workspace Monday.** 20 minutes is enough. If you don't
  open it, the memory doesn't accumulate and the product can't earn
  its keep.
- **Capture outcomes.** Even just "Sent" on three contacts a week is
  enough to start the loop. Empty weeks are fine occasionally; empty
  months mean the workspace isn't right for you.
- **Tell the founder if something feels wrong.** A wrong opener, a
  missed contact, an outcome that didn't change next week's brief —
  those are bugs and we fix them. Don't quietly stop opening the
  email.

---

## What you should NOT do

- Don't expect the workspace to "explain" why someone is on the list
  beyond what your own CRM said. The product doesn't know more than
  your data.
- Don't expect property records, role updates, or other external
  intelligence in your first month. Those exist as substrate but are
  only surfaced if your contact data quality supports them safely.
  We'll never present a fact we can't cite.
- Don't expect a dashboard or analytics. There aren't any. The product
  is the Monday brief and the workspace it links to.
- Don't share your login. Each workspace is per-operator. If a team
  member needs access, ask for a separate workspace.

---

## What to do if something looks wrong

- **A name is wrong** ("Greg · Greg") — tell the founder. It's an
  import bug; the founder fixes it in under a day.
- **A priority looks irrelevant** — note which contact and why. The
  ranking is deterministic; if it's wrong, the inputs need
  inspection.
- **The brief is generic or "AI-feeling"** — this should not happen.
  Report it immediately; the product has explicit guards against
  fluffy or templated language and any regression is a high-priority
  fix.
- **You can't sign in** — text the founder. Don't fight the login
  screen.

---

## What success looks like, from your side

- You open the Monday brief and within 30 seconds you know what to
  do today.
- You spend 20 minutes Monday morning making three or four real
  reach-outs against the priority list.
- You capture an outcome on each.
- You return Wednesday and see your remaining priorities organized
  cleanly.
- By Week 4, you couldn't go back to your previous CRM-only flow.
- By Week 12, you've reactivated relationships you'd written off,
  and at least one of them produced a closed deal that wouldn't have
  happened without the weekly nudge.

If most of that doesn't happen by Week 8, the workspace isn't fitting
your business. That's a conversation we'll have honestly; we won't
chase you for another month of payment.
