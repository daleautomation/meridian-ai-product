# Meridian — Operator Pricing

> One page. Founder-led. No platform, no AI hype, no tiers.

---

## What you get every week

A Monday-morning brief in your inbox at 7 AM, with a workspace link.
The workspace shows up to eight priority relationships for the week.
Every priority cites the CRM evidence behind it. You click outcomes
inline as you work. Next Monday, the brief reflects what you did.

That's the product.

## What you do not get

- An AI that talks at you.
- A predictive score for "likely to close."
- A new CRM to migrate to.
- A dashboard with engagement metrics.
- Outbound automation.
- Real-time alerts.
- A mobile app.

Those are the products you've already tried. This is not that.

## What this costs

| Plan | Monthly | One-time onboarding | Commitment |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Solo operator** (residential agent, single workspace) | **$499 / month** | $500 | 60 days |
| **Specialty contractor** (single vertical, single workspace) | **$1,499 / month** | $1,000 | 60 days |
| **Small team** (up to 5 workspaces, single vertical) | $2,500 / month | $1,500 | 60 days |

Founder-led delivery for the first 60 days. No self-serve.

## What the onboarding fee covers

- CSV import + column mapping
- Workspace tuning to your vertical
- Three weeks of Monday-morning briefs delivered by the founder
- Three founder check-ins to review what the brief surfaced
- One CRM-rehab pass: surnames and addresses backfilled with you, live

## What the monthly fee covers

- Weekly Monday brief, delivered every week.
- Workspace access for the assigned operator(s).
- Outcome capture, with continuity memory accumulating week-over-week.
- One founder hour per month for adjustment and review.

## What it does NOT cover

- Re-imports beyond the first onboarding.
- Custom integrations (Zapier, automation, webhooks).
- Marketing or outreach content.
- Multi-vertical expansion within the same workspace.

## How payment works

- Invoice sent Tuesday of Week 2, after the second Monday brief lands.
- Net 7 terms. Pay by ACH or check. No Stripe surcharge.
- 60-day commitment is binding. Refund after 60 days is full month
  pro-rated; no refund for the first two months.

## Cancellation

Email the founder. The current month finishes; the next month does not
invoice. The workspace data remains yours — you can request a JSON
export of all your contacts + captured outcomes at any time.

## Why these prices

The work is intentionally founder-led. There is no salesperson, no
support tier, no procurement deck. You're paying for one specific
weekly outcome (the brief), measured against one specific weekly
behavior (you captured outcomes that compound into next week's
priorities). If that outcome doesn't materialize after eight weeks,
the price isn't supportable for either of us.

---

## When to walk away from this offer

- If you want a new CRM, this isn't it.
- If you want an AI that emails your clients, this isn't it.
- If you want predictive lead scores, this isn't it.
- If your existing CRM is empty, this isn't it (we read your data, we don't replace it).
- If you can't commit to checking the workspace weekly, the moat
  doesn't form and you'll feel like you're paying for nothing.

The product is for operators who already know who matters in their
list and need help remembering when. If that's not your problem, save
the $499.
